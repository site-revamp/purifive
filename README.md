# PuriFive Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

PuriFive (PuriFive Senses) is a Dubai-based indoor environmental solutions company specialising in indoor air quality assessment, environmental monitoring, ventilation, and air purification.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating PuriFive's assessment-led approach, explaining its environmental solutions more clearly, and helping residential and commercial customers understand the value of improving their indoor environments.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Homepage Hero Messaging
- Replaced the broad positioning statement with more specific messaging focused on indoor air quality assessment and environmental performance.
- Made PuriFive's core services and customer value clearer from the first section.
- Strengthened the connection between environmental assessment and tailored solutions.

### 2. Reduced Repetitive Homepage Content
- Addressed repeated FAQ and environmental assessment messaging.
- Improved content hierarchy and section organisation.
- Reduced unnecessary repetition to give important service information and conversion sections greater prominence.

### 3. More Understandable Environmental Monitoring Data
- Improved the presentation of environmental dashboard information.
- Added clearer context to scores, percentages, and air-quality indicators.
- Focused on helping visitors understand what the displayed measurements represent and why they matter.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Established a clearer visual hierarchy to communicate PuriFive's indoor environmental expertise.
- **Layout:** Improved section organisation and reduced repetitive content throughout the homepage.
- **User experience:** Made the assessment-led service approach easier to understand and follow.
- **Data presentation:** Improved the clarity and context of environmental monitoring information.
- **Overall appearance:** Created a more focused and professional homepage direction that prioritises service understanding, environmental insights, and customer enquiries.

## Project Objective

The redesign addresses three critical issues identified during the original website research: unclear homepage messaging, repetitive content, and unexplained environmental monitoring data. The overall objective is to communicate PuriFive's value more effectively and make its indoor environmental solutions easier for customers to understand.
