Understand Your Environment

What Happens Inside Your Air?

Indoor environmental assessment considers multiple factors together. Each measurement helps build a more complete understanding of the conditions within the areas assessed.

Airborne Particles

PM2.5 and PM10 measurements help assess fine and larger airborne particles.

Carbon Dioxide

CO₂ patterns help explain occupancy, fresh-air exchange and ventilation behaviour.

Indoor Gases

TVOC readings indicate changes in total volatile organic compounds.

Humidity

Understand moisture conditions and patterns that can affect indoor comfort.

Temperature

Examine temperature conditions alongside other environmental measurements.

Airflow

Accessible airflow observations help identify distribution and ventilation concerns.
